Ceneo Web Scraper

Ceneo Web Scraper is an application that allows the analysis of product reviews from the Ceneo.pl website. The user enters the Product ID, and the tool retrieves detailed review information and generates two interactive charts:

  • Rating Distribution (number of stars) – shows how many reviews were given for each rating (from 1 to 5 stars).
  • Sentiment Analysis of Reviews – shows the number of positive, neutral, and negative reviews.

How to Use

1. Go to Ceneo.pl and select the products you are interested in.

2. Save the product IDs.

3. Go to the "Search Reviews" tab and enter the saved product IDs.

4. To compare all searched products, go to the "Product List" tab.
